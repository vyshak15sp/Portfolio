Project Detail

Study on Tamil Nadu's Single Window Portal

Internship project at Guidance Tamil Nadu focused on evaluating the state's single window facilitation system and improving industrial approval workflows through policy and process analysis.

Download Report
Organization: Guidance Tamil Nadu Internship Period: April 15 to June 6, 2025 Program: PGDM Summer Internship (RBS)

Problem

Problem Statement

The study was conducted as part of my summer internship at Guidance Tamil Nadu, the state's investment facilitation agency. The Single Window Portal was designed to streamline approvals by integrating over 20 departments, while complementary initiatives like Biz Buddy support grievance redressal for industries.

Approach

Research Approach

Assessed the portal's effectiveness for industrial approvals using structured primary research, issue-level analysis, and recommendation mapping to improve investor experience, timelines, and operational transparency.

Study Design

  • Primary data collected from 22 companies that used the portal.
  • Coverage included users from 2024, 2023, 2022, and earlier cohorts.
  • Problem-centred research framework with data analysis and interpretation phases.
  • Findings structured into actionable suggestions and conclusion sections.

Tools & Methods Used

Survey Analysis Policy Benchmarking Process Mapping Excel Stakeholder Feedback

Evaluation Parameters

  • Portal ease of use and overall investor experience rating.
  • Department interaction quality and timeliness of updates.
  • Delay in approvals and primary causes behind delay.
  • Average approval time and help-desk support effectiveness.
  • User-reported improvement priorities.

Key Contributions During Internship

  • Interpreted survey outputs and converted them into issue-wise insights.
  • Prepared structured summaries for policy and process review discussions.
  • Connected user-experience gaps with workflow-level improvement suggestions.

Recommendations & Outcome

  • Reinforced the need for stronger inter-department coordination and status visibility.
  • Highlighted approval-delay drivers and service-quality gaps requiring intervention.
  • Provided practical inputs to improve portal responsiveness and user support quality.
  • Built a data-backed perspective aligned with investor-centric governance goals.
